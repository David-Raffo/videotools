## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-24

### Added

- Multi-track timeline with split, trim, ripple delete, speed changes and undo/redo.
- Original audio lane with volume envelope plus multiple music and voice tracks.
- Color grading with built-in looks, custom `.cube` LUTs and real-time WebGL2 preview.
- Export to MP4, WebM, MKV, GIF and MP3 with CRF or target size, 10-bit and VAAPI support.
- HDR to SDR tone mapping, subtitles, crop, rotation and frame-rate conversion.
- Resumable chunked uploads with instant local preview while uploading.
- Quick drone edit batch mode.
- Docker and Docker Compose deployment with optional GPU override.
